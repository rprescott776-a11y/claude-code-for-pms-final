# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.
Is there more detail about the callouts being gone before the responder can act and do we know how common this is?

### 2.
what is the live callout and why is it hard to miss?

### 3.
what do the interviewee's think of the change in the ping wait from 90s to 60s?

### 4.
Would the interviewee's like more time to answer the ping before it moves on?

### 5.
Based on the interviewee feedback, what changed with this release?

### 6.
Please group the types of tickets by who created them

### 7.
please exclude tickets that do not appear to be related to the most recent release

### 8.
what are the Open tickets related to?

### 9.
Match each responder's ping count to their handler's ticket count

### 10.
Was there are change to filtering that could impact who is pinged?

### 11.
does proximity come up in the interviews or tickets?

### 12.
Compare the ranking weights before and after 4.2
