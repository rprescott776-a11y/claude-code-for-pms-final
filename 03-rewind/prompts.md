# 03 · Rewind — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly.

Last session you read four conversations and every support ticket
since 4.2 — and found the two piles did not agree.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

Break the missed pings out by responder

### 2.

Show the same split week by week and please share this in a graph format

### 3.

Please graph out response time for the 4 most impacted responders both before and after the release

### 4.

compare availability to pings for the responders

### 5.

Compare availability against # of pings before and after release

### 6.

rank missed pings by responder by most to least pings before the release

### 7.

Compare the support_tickets table on rook-database against the pings table. Do they agree with each other? When did people start writing in, and when do the numbers actually move?

### 8.

Graph the weekly side by side table - instead of Missed pings by percentage, just graph #

### 9.

Are the people who are reporting the tickets the people who we are seeing the issues with?

### 10.

Let's look at Farlight specifically. Show me every week for them. How many times were they pinged and how many calls they took. What can you tell me happened with Farlight week by week in plain english.

### 11.

Once someone is low on the pings received is there anyway for them to recover?
